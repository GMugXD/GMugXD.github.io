Crawlers

Robo spider mounts. The equivalent of the horse; the stallion of Voskovia. Crawlers are a machination lifeform which resembles gigantic spiders; multi legged, multi eyed, but with armour maijims and fibernetic fur. They are around the same size as horses but can be bigger or smaller, depending on the exact species or set of crawler. Despite their size, they are excellent scalers and use a special type of meiss they create and harness anti-gravity to help scale larger obstacles. They are also terrifyingly fast.

Crawlers sustain themselves on literally anything (matter->pen) except arte (which they dislike), and bond with each other or their caretaker. They can also survive basically anywhere because of their nature as a machination. For these reasons, voskovians trained, domesticated, bred, and utilised crawlers as mounts, as pets, and as weapons and emplacements Crawlers reproduce by using a similar technique to Compositoration, but by their special meiss.

They excel better in space/ non-atmospheric environments, and are smart, loyal creatures. Truly, they are a voskovian's best friend.

Flak

Cute space machine butterfly-birds. Very small and social machination lifeforms that resemble butterflies, with six wings, two eyes, and mouths with mandibles that produce rudimentary meiological lasers to cut and harvest minerals from erisei, which is their natural environment, but they have also thrived in voskovian settlements and grids, hitching rides on vessels and sometimes eating them, creating those triangular mark textures that their mouths make, and that most vessels which have seen extensive use possess. Grids, specifically forgegrids and similar, are one of the places where they thrive exceptionally well and are deeply welcomed, feeding on excess redundant or useless, subpar fysen.

The flaks' also produce special flak meiss "enzymes" that reinforce, harden and chemically densify—contract—synthetic fysen, when it comes into contact with heat or energy, like it does when they use their lasers to nibble. This literally enhances and reinforces otherwise subpar materials, which are nibbled by a drome of flak. Flak also output by-products and excess minerals, in the form of dark, reflective meiss crystals called fayn, which is extremely desirable and applied in a variety of applications for many reasons.

Their physical maijims that produce rudimentary, focused lasers are the same concept used by VCM lances and erisei mining lasers. Additionally, their appearance is extremely striking, as their wings resemble ymira fragments, and with metallic, almost vantastel for their bodies. Flak bond with voskovians and some voskovians keep entire dromes of flak as pets.

TACIT VOSKOVA
Walking down the hallways of the estate, my eyes drift toward the large, glass pane windows of intricate colour. They made up most of the wall. Stopping, and peering closer, I saw the multilayered mass cityscape of Val Voskova. Pitch gray skies and clouds of automata circled the settlement, as the countless technological constructs Voskovia had built came into view.
I took some time looking. Thinking. It was.. improbable, for a civilisation like ours to exist-no-let alone thrive. In the face of the many perils the creators sent, we thrive. "Resilience.", I thought.
But it couldn't put me at ease. I looked behind me, and there was nothing. I knew that.
No longer peering, I kept walking to where I was going beforehand: my quarters. After entering through the set of doors that was the royal gate, i sat down on the well.. extravagant fabric lining my bed, as much as it displeased me. I needed some time to think for other options, other ways for this to end. My mind - old as is, still clinged to the youth that was once mine.
A knock came from right outside the door. "Your majesty, would you care for some tea?", came from one of the servants.
"No.", I firmly rejected. It was not time for me to numb myself and accept it like I always did.
My mind was already numb from the duties and roles i had to play-partake as emperor. Was I ready to truly accept the terms that were presented to me.. the terms I sought, which in retrospect.. only turned to confirm the calling of-
A thunderstrike jolted me, as i instinctively unsheathed my katerina. I fell down, onto my knees, looking up — at the sky through the window. "To be so paranoid..", a voice told me, a voice that came from my mouth, but i couldn't recognise it as my own.
I was losing it.
I only had the slightest thought, the slightest connection — of what could the signs of the days before. With the terms I sought only confirming the ending.. i had no path to walk except the one fate had in store.
I propped myself up with the Katerina in hand, only to fall back down as a sense of unease made me tense. It crept up me like a water that flowed in reverse.. myrids that slithered up me.
The world around me was darkened, and I felt my vision cloud. I propped myself up using the bed and katerina, finally getting up. I looked around, and heard.. nothing. And when I listened, I saw the skies tint a brilliant red. My senses were warping.. in a way I had never seen, and i tensed in a way that I had never felt.
I walked toward the mirror in my quarters and saw a face i didn't recognise. Instinctively, i grabbed my cape and used it as a kinetic striker to shatter the mirror into millions of brilliant fragments. But I still felt the stare, and so i turned to face a shadow, wrapping itself around me like one fulms a fabric. I felt my throat tighten, as the shadow held me in its grasp, before i defied and swiped it in a brilliant, white slash with my katerina.
It would weave, and move quickly, trying to jab me with something before i grabbed a lamp, and caved the lamp onto it. I would see it struggle, before i used my cape to slam the bookshelf down under.
What, was happening?
I felt my hands defy me as I let go of my katerina, stumbling out and falling on my back, out of my quarters. Reality felt like it warped around me, conforming to whatever shape it saw fit..
Propping myself back up, I realised I couldn't stay still. I had to move. And in the state of shock, my wits came back to me as i considered the options I had left while moving down the hallway. Shredding my cape into it's individual strings of brilliant fabric, i cut my hair using the strings while i realised one thing. There was no path left me to go, except to carve my own.
I quickly turned a corner and looked, as I heard shadows that stared at me with the nothing they had. I would walk past them, as they would all bow much to my confusion.

The hallways were all full of shadows that stared at me, and each one would bow. There was no face I could recognise, as i would enter a ceremonial room and saw more like them.
I felt the same flowing sensation, as it flowed downwards. I would feel no relief as I fell down again, where the shadows would slowly move toward me. They would suffocate me, as my throat, chest and mind itself tighten, and tense. As I looked up, a shadow would stare directly into me.
I accepted its grasp, using it to prop myself up, before stumbling back, and swiping it with the strings of my cape. And again, again, and again. I couldn't stand being suffocated.. i..
"What are you.. all doing.." i let out under pained breaths. "What.. are you.. all?" i would let out once more. And i felt my ears tighten as i tried to see the shadow, who had fallen onto its knees. I saw nothing but my own voice, as the world turned deafingly silent. The light that was cast onto the room from the glass panes had turned red, contrasting the world that had now turned colourless.
The shadows blocked my path. Grabbing a hold of my scattered thoughts, connecting them with string.. i remembered I had to carve a path.
"For no one but me.". Words that I felt come from my mouth, yet i felt no recognition of my own voice. There stood a statue of the first emperor of Voskovia, who reunited the regions of voskovia, and marked the start of the current permidan eracycle. Here he stood, with his katerina in hand. I shot all the shadows who stood in my way to the first emperor, reloading the shells in the cardigan as i arrived.
I kicked the pedestal the statue stood in one swift motion, and grabbed the Katerina of the first emperor, as the statue would crumble into the meiss and asta that it was already degraded into. The Katerina would be my key to carving the path for me.
And to all that stood in my way, including the shadows that now cowered in my presence.. i will have to slay them using the will of Voskovia.

Arks

Sniper railcannon birds. The ark operates alone, biding it time patiently, as it waits for the perfect opportunity to strike, all while branding itself in the stell's fire of environmental hazards. With such a way of living almost analogous to one of Voskovia, the Ark has become a symbol deeply rooted in voskovian culture.

Arks are notable for their accelerator—salvager maijims that they use to hunt and nail other creatures and vessels, before sweeping in with their scaled wings, which they use to shield and camouflage themselves. Using the same maijim, they strike and consume the raw, metallic debris / scrap to sustain themselves. The wings of an Ark are used mainly to shield itself from the environment, being durable and constantly being shed to achieve that. When shed, the scales on the wings reveal themselves as the small, sharp blades they really are, which is an extra offensive measure Arks can take.
The Ark is an extremely fast creature, able to accelerate onto speeds comparable to vessels, chasing them down. While Arks do scavenge dead machinations for sustenance, the materials they consume directly correlate to the quality of their accelerator shots and wing scales, which has led to their preference shifting to the vessels of the Voskovia.

Arcplasm

A subset of the machination lifeforms. They are best described as holographic blocky, machination neoplasm which grows rapidly, harvest and refine pure energy (pen)energy (pen)]]. Their method of refining and harvesting pen varies from colony to colony, of which Arcplasm possess special meiological components (called meijims) that allow them to run simulations and processing operations, powered by pen to increase efficiency of pen refining, and to develop entire new methods of pen harvesting.
The identified methods of pen harvesting are known as the arcplasms' Arcs, and utilise extremely meticulous but efficient chains of harvesting, refining mechanical organs (meijims), which are distributed and used to expand their operations and power their simulation meijims.

Arcplasm colonies can stretch and cover entire pryi and erisei, and can combine and merge with other colonies to form megacolonies that either keep their separate arcplasm colony identity but share processing power and methods they have found, or merge into one colony altogether. This can happen even between different species of arcplasm, which there are many. They also possess a rudimentary method of communication using binary data via the Datcosm, the flow of pen, or other methods.

They spread to other pryi and erisei by collisions and other celestial events. Arcplasm colonies appear like holographic stretches of metallic dark gray, with their maijims and other processes being tall, almosted pixelated spires. Arcplasm colonies also like to organise themselves, which combined with the aforementioned traits, make colonies resemble massive stretches of three dimensional, living mechanical motherboards/CPUs.

Myrids

A type of machination lifeform which resembles fearsome worm-squids: slithering, erisei boreing and wandering the Stell. Their appearance is comparable to strings/cables; their body being long, while remaining disproportionately thin. The body of the Myrid grows at the ends, becoming smaller and thinner until it becomes the branching multiples of tails at their ends.

They are fearsome hunters, attacking vessels or machinations (even other myrids) to consume their workings and arte, which they sustain pen from. They aren't particularly smart, but are still highly dangerous because of their method of attack, where they bore into erisei, travel with them, and then using their senses, they jump prey.
Myrids use their body and completely wrap around prey in breakneck speeds, imploding them as they sustain themselves. Their branching, slithering tails are also used to deflect projectiles and defend themselves when needed.

While myrids like killing eachother, they can also work together, with smaller myrids wrapping around and coiling up with bigger myrids, and dispenhe pen they gather, forming Myriads.

Myrids possess no sight, hearing, taste or any of the sort. They only possess the sense of signals, which allows them to sense data signals, communication and anything via the datcosm as if seeing it.
Additionally, as Myrids cannot die of age, they are effectively immortal unless starved or killed.

The scales of the Myrids make them desirable for some applications, with their tails being effectively indestructible, on par with Tantameist or other military exclusive composites. The secte of myridtending, which is where myrids are copena the feeding of pen and other practises, to create somewhat obedient myrids that are deployed in limited uses in things like military or civilian, for their scales or power.

Some houses are built on only one ideal secte with myridtending being no exception, as there are many major houses who solely specialise in said secte.
